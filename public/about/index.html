About Open Systems Lab#

Open Systems Lab is a specialized AI infrastructure consulting firm dedicated to helping organizations bridge the gap between traditional infrastructure automation and modern AI-powered solutions. We combine deep expertise in DevOps, cloud platforms, and emerging AI technologies to deliver transformational results for our clients.

Our Mission#

We believe that the future of infrastructure lies in intelligent automation that adapts, learns, and optimizes continuously. Our mission is to make AI-powered infrastructure accessible to organizations of all sizes, enabling them to achieve operational excellence while reducing complexity and costs.

Our Approach#

AI-First Infrastructure Design#

We don’t just add AI as an afterthought—we design infrastructure solutions with AI capabilities built into their foundation. This approach enables natural language operations, intelligent decision-making, and automated optimization from day one.

Proven Methodologies#

Our solutions are built on battle-tested DevOps and GitOps principles, enhanced with modern AI capabilities. We leverage established tools and frameworks while incorporating cutting-edge AI technologies to create robust, scalable solutions.

Security and Compliance First#

Every solution we deliver incorporates security best practices from the ground up. We understand the critical importance of maintaining compliance and security posture while innovating with new technologies.

Knowledge Transfer and Sustainability#

We don’t just implement solutions—we ensure your team can maintain, extend, and optimize them. Our comprehensive training programs and documentation ensure long-term success and independence.

Cloud & Infrastructure#

Technology Stack#

We work with cutting-edge technologies across the entire infrastructure and AI spectrum:

AI & Machine Learning: Model Context Protocol, Anthropic Claude Integration, LLM Operations, Generative AI Workflows

Cloud Platforms: AWS (EC2, VPC, S3, IAM, CloudWatch), Azure (Storage, Compute, PIM, Active Directory)

Programming Languages: Go (microservices), TypeScript, Python, Shell scripting

Observability: Prometheus, Grafana, Loki, OpenTelemetry, Jaeger

Security: DevSecOps scanning, container security, vulnerability assessment, compliance automation
